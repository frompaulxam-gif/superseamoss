BEFORE YOU ORDER

Know your jar.

How do I order?+

Build your mix-and-match order above. Choose One-off order or Subscribe & Save here, then copy your blend choices and click Continue to Square to open the matching checkout. Paste your blends into the required blend-choice field, add your delivery details and pay securely. For one-off orders, keep the quantity at 1. If Square shows a purchase option, select One-time purchase. For subscriptions, enter your full UK mainland delivery name, address and postcode in the required field.

How much is delivery?+

Chilled UK mainland shipping is £9.95. Local delivery: ask us on Instagram. One-off orders get free delivery when the jar subtotal after discounts is £50 or more, excluding postage. Monthly subscriptions placed through our Subscribe & Save checkout include free UK mainland delivery, with no minimum spend.

When will my jars arrive?+

We make every jar fresh. Please allow 3 to 4 working days after your order is confirmed for preparation and dispatch. You’ll receive an email when your jars are on their way. Next-day shipping starts after dispatch, rather than when you place your order.

Can I subscribe?+

Yes. The monthly option gives you 10% off your jars and can be cancelled anytime. Choose Subscribe & Save above to open the dedicated monthly checkout with free UK mainland delivery. Paste your blend choices and enter your full delivery address in the required fields. Check the monthly total before subscribing.

Where is your Seamoss sourced?+

Our Seamoss is always organically sourced from St Lucia and Jamaica. We have a relationship with the fisherman through our supplier.

What’s inside each blend?+

Open a blend above to see the ingredients featured in its original Instagram post. Ask the team for the full current ingredient and allergen information before ordering.

Are all the blends vegan?+

Manuka Glow contains manuka honey. Check your chosen blend’s current ingredients with the team if you follow a vegan diet.

How do I use and store my jar?+

Take 1–3 dessert spoons daily, on its own or added to smoothies, juices, yoghurt or other foods. Keep refrigerated at all times and consume within 10–14 days of opening. Always use a clean, dry spoon and return to the refrigerator immediately after use.

Which blend is suitable for me?+

Check the ingredients and blend-specific guidance before ordering. The brand advises against Rich Clarification during pregnancy, and All Night Long during pregnancy or if you have heart problems. Ask a qualified health professional if you need advice about your circumstances.

Can I use these blends during pregnancy or breastfeeding?+

Manuka Glow (Expecting Mother Edition) is the only blend we offer for expectant mothers. It contains Seamoss, Manuka honey and lemon, with no maca or ginseng. This is a separate recipe from our original Manuka Glow. Check the full ingredients with your GP, midwife or pharmacist before use to confirm it is suitable for you, especially if you are unsure. If you are breastfeeding, ask your GP, midwife or pharmacist before using any blend. Avoid blends containing ashwagandha during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
