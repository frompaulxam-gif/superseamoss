What happens next

  1. Keep your Square confirmation

    Your Square receipt confirms your payment and order details. Check your inbox and junk folder. If checkout didn’t finish or you’re unsure whether payment went through, contact us before trying again.

  2. Made fresh, for you

    Once your order is confirmed, please allow 3 to 4 working days for preparation and dispatch. We’ll email you when your jars are on their way. Next-day shipping starts after dispatch.

  3. Make it your moment

    When your jars arrive, follow the serving and storage guidance on your jar. Keep them refrigerated and use a clean, dry spoon.
